# Consent Management

> Draft—requires review by qualified legal, privacy, security, and healthcare professionals before production use.

Consent must be specific, informed, granular, versioned, reversible, and separate from account acceptance. Record actor, purpose, scope, version, time, region, withdrawal, and downstream revocation. Never infer consent from silence.
