# Government and law-enforcement request policy

Route requests to trained Legal; support staff neither disclose nor confirm data. Verify identity, authority, scope, jurisdiction and emergency basis; seek narrowing; preserve only required data; minimize disclosure; log access; and notify the user unless lawfully prohibited or unsafe. Emergency requests receive expedited counsel review, not automatic disclosure. Track holds separately from routine retention and lift them when authorized. Publish aggregate transparency information only after privacy/legal review.
