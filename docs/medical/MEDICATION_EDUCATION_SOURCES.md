# Medication education sources

Adapters identify DailyMed/NLM, FDA drug labeling, and MedlinePlus government sources. Every record requires medication identifier, organization, title, source URL/identifier, publication/revision and retrieval dates, region, common uses, warnings, contraindications, limitations, human-review status, and re-review date. Adapters are readiness scaffolding, not enabled content feeds.

Content must be traceable and human reviewed. It cannot provide individualized dosing, recommend treatment, turn label text into personalized advice, imply FDA approval of Mona’s Heart, or contain unsupported claims.
