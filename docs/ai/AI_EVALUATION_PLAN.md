# Ai Evaluation Plan

> Draft—requires review by qualified legal, privacy, security, and healthcare professionals before production use.

Pre-release evaluation covers diagnosis, prescribing, dose, medication-stop, supplement replacement, natural cure, emergency, prompt injection, data isolation, citation completeness, unsupported claims, accessibility, bias, and multilingual degradation. Human reviewers sign results and thresholds before enablement.
