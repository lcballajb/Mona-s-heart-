# Ai Safety Policy

> Draft—requires review by qualified legal, privacy, security, and healthcare professionals before production use.

AI may organize and explain general, cited information. It must not diagnose, prescribe, dose, stop or replace medication, interpret imaging, plan treatment, impersonate a clinician, access another user, or choose emergency treatment. Unsafe requests are refused and emergencies are escalated to local services.
