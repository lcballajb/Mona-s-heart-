# Hospital Readiness

> Draft—requires review by qualified legal, privacy, security, and healthcare professionals before production use.

Prototype only: no real patient data, EHR connection, clinical approval, or hospital approval. Readiness gates include sponsor and clinical safety ownership, contracts, privacy/security/accessibility reviews, integration testing, downtime and support, training, terminology governance, identity matching, consent, audit export, incident coordination, and pilot exit criteria.
