# Terminology update policy

Terminology imports are staged, never auto-published. The Data Governance owner records license, edition, checksum, retrieval time, upstream URI, region and provenance; scans input; validates schema, retired codes, mappings, duplicates and sample clinical cases. Clinical/Pharmacy reviews meaning and contraindication-sensitive changes. Engineering runs backward-compatibility and rollback tests. Two-person approval promotes a pinned snapshot; prior snapshots remain restorable. Emergency safety corrections are reviewed before display.
