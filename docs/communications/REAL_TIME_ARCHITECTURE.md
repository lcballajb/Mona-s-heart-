# Real Time Architecture

> Draft—requires review by qualified legal, privacy, security, and healthcare professionals before production use.

Future messaging, presence, typing, notifications, moderator/coordinator alerts, and WebRTC signaling require authenticated short-lived connections, object authorization, rate limits, abuse controls, retention boundaries, audit events, and tenant isolation. Video design includes waiting room, camera/microphone controls, end, block, report, reschedule, cancel, time zones, and no recording by default. Do not claim end-to-end encryption without verification.
