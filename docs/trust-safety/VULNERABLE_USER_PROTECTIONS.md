# Vulnerable user protections

Use private defaults, least disclosure, accessible consent, friction before sharing/contact, block/report, scam and coercion signals, trusted-contact choices without surveillance, and trained human escalation. Never expose diagnosis/location in discovery. High-risk matching, messaging and video remain disabled pending abuse-case testing. Avoid dark patterns and dependency on a caregiver; support revocation and confidential access. Consult disability, older-adult, survivor and patient communities with Privacy/Clinical/Trust & Safety oversight.
