# Misinformation policy

Disallow content likely to cause serious health harm, fraudulent cures, dangerous dosing/interactions, impersonated clinicians and predatory sales. Use evidence severity/context, not viewpoint popularity. Label or reduce lower-risk disputed claims; remove/escalate imminent-danger content; preserve satire/personal experience when clearly framed. Clinical reviewers decide medical accuracy using current authoritative sources; provide reasons and appeal. Never auto-publish corrections or AI health text.
