# Policy control standard

> **Draft—requires review by qualified legal, privacy, security, healthcare, and regulatory professionals before production use.**

This is the canonical lifecycle shared by individually approved policy files. Each policy must retain its own version, effective/review date, change summary, approval status/evidence, approver role, notification and re-consent decision, and archived predecessor. Common language here must not replace policy-specific facts.

1. Product provides verified features, users, entities, jurisdictions, data inventory/flows, vendors and UI behavior.
2. Accountable domain owners draft; qualified reviewers decide applicability and required disclosures without relying on automation.
3. Engineering and QA demonstrate that the product, consent, rights, accessibility, retention and safety behavior matches the reviewed draft.
4. Approvers record scoped, dated evidence in the legal review log and policy registry. “Reviewed” is not “approved.”
5. Release owners determine accessible/localized notice, effective time, material-change notification, re-consent, refusal/withdrawal and rollback.
6. Archive the predecessor immutably. Never auto-publish policy changes and never overwrite history.
