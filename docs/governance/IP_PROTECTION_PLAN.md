# Intellectual-property protection plan

No trademark registration, patent protection or copyright registration is claimed. Counsel should verify domain ownership, conduct clearance searches, set trademark filing/usage strategy, assess patentability before public disclosure, evaluate copyright registration, maintain contributor invention/IP assignments and employee/contractor confidentiality, and approve open-source contribution/licensing policy. Preserve provenance for code/content/design/clinical sources and do not use third-party material beyond license. Review annually.
