# Insurance and risk transfer plan

No coverage is claimed. With qualified broker/counsel, assess cyber/privacy, technology E&O, general liability, D&O, media/IP, crime and clinical/professional exposures. Map limits, exclusions, retention, territorial scope, vendor incidents, ransomware, regulatory defense, notification vendors and contractual requirements to the risk register. Test incident notification to carriers and avoid admissions that prejudice coverage. Review annually and after material product/data/jurisdiction changes.

## Document boundary and cross-references

Insurance planning only; it neither confirms coverage nor accepts/transfers a repository risk. Use the [risk register](RISK_REGISTER.md), [vendor register](VENDOR_REGISTER.md), and [90-day plan](90_DAY_ACTION_PLAN.md).
