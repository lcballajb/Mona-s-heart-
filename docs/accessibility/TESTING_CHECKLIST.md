# Testing Checklist

> Draft—requires review by qualified legal, privacy, security, and healthcare professionals before production use.

Test keyboard-only paths, focus order/restoration, headings/landmarks, labels/instructions/errors, listbox announcements, 200% and 400% zoom, small viewport reflow, color contrast, reduced motion, touch targets, VoiceOver, NVDA, and automated scans. Record browser, AT, result, severity, and remediation.
