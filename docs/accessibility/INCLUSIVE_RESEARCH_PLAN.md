# Inclusive research plan

Recruit with compensated community partners across disability, age, literacy, language, device and bandwidth—not as proxies for one another. Obtain accessible consent, minimize sensitive data, offer remote/in-person accommodations, permit support persons, and provide withdrawal. Do not recruit minors until minors mode and ethics/privacy review are approved. Publish themes without identifying participants; log barriers, severity, owner and retest. Independent accessibility and research-ethics review remain required.
