# Integrative health review policy

Herbs, supplements, TCM, Ayurveda, acupuncture and spiritual/wellness claims require an Integrative Health reviewer plus Clinical or Pharmacy review. Separate traditional use from clinical evidence; record identity/preparation/dose, product variability, region, pregnancy/age cautions, interactions, adverse effects and regulator warnings. Never imply “natural” means safe, endorse sellers, replace treatment, or publish AI-generated recommendations. Review at least quarterly and immediately upon a safety signal.
