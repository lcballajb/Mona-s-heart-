# De-identification limitations

Removing direct identifiers does not make health, location, free text, rare conditions, images, voice or longitudinal records anonymous. Linkage, membership inference and small-cell risks persist. Document method and jurisdiction, expert/statistical review where required, residual risk, prohibited linkage, recipient controls, minimum cohorts, query/output controls and destruction. “De-identified” is not a blanket exemption or permission for secondary use. Re-test when datasets, recipients or external data change.
