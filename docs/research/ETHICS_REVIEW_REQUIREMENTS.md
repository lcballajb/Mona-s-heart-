# Ethics review requirements

No recruitment, analysis or sharing until the designated official records IRB/ethics approval, exemption or non-research determination; repository staff must not self-declare it. Evidence includes protocol/version, consent, sites/regions, vulnerable populations, conflicts, investigators, security, compensation, withdrawal, adverse events, data-sharing/publication and continuing review. Amendments pause affected activity until approved. Algorithm studies require subgroup performance, bias, clinical significance and incident monitoring.
